New York City is home to some of the most famous attractions in the world. These landmarks represent the culture, history, and energy of the city.

Central Park

Central Park, located in Manhattan between 59th and 110th Street, includes well-known spots such as Bethesda Terrace, Bow Bridge, and the Central Park Zoo. Visitors can walk, bike, rent boats at the lake, or relax on the Great Lawn.

Times Square

Times Square, located in Midtown Manhattan, is known for its large digital billboards, Broadway theaters, and constant crowds. Visitors often take photos, watch street performers, and attend Broadway shows nearby.

Brooklyn Bridge

The Brooklyn Bridge connects Lower Manhattan to Brooklyn and offers views of the Manhattan skyline and East River. Walking or biking across the bridge is a popular activity, especially for photos of the city.

Museums

The Metropolitan Museum of Art, located on the Upper East Side, features famous works such as ancient Egyptian artifacts, European paintings, and the Temple of Dendur. The Museum of Modern Art in Midtown includes well-known works like Van Gogh’s Starry Night and modern art collections.
