Food for Every Budget

New York City is known for its diverse and high-quality food scene. You can find almost any type of cuisine, from quick street food to high-end dining. New York City offers food options for every budget, from quick and affordable meals to high-end dining experiences. No matter how much you want to spend, you can find great food throughout the city.

Budget Eats ($)

Joe's Pizza

Joe’s Pizza, located in Greenwich Village in Manhattan, is known for its classic New York-style slices, especially its plain cheese slice and pepperoni slice. The thin, foldable crust and quick service make it a popular stop for both locals and visitors looking for an authentic NYC pizza experience.

Tompkins Square Bagels

Tompkins Square Bagels, located in the East Village, is known for its fresh bagels and large portions. Popular choices include bagels with scallion cream cheese, bacon-egg-and-cheese sandwiches, and lox with cream cheese. The shop is especially busy in the mornings with both locals and tourists.

The Halal Guys

The Halal Guys started as a street cart on 53rd Street and 6th Avenue in Midtown Manhattan. They are best known for their chicken and rice platter, gyro platter, and their signature white sauce and hot sauce, which have made them one of the most famous street food vendors in the city.

Mid-Range Dining ($$)

Rubirosa

Rubirosa, located in Nolita, is known for its thin-crust pizzas and Italian dishes. One of its most popular items is the vodka pizza, along with classic pasta dishes like rigatoni and meatballs. The restaurant is popular for both casual meals and group dining.

Jack's Wife Freda

Jack’s Wife Freda, with locations in SoHo and the West Village, is known for its Mediterranean-inspired menu. Popular dishes include the green shakshuka, rosewater waffles, and avocado toast. It is especially known as a trendy brunch destination.

Luxury Dining ($$$)

Carbone

Carbone, located in Greenwich Village, is a high-end Italian restaurant known for its famous spicy rigatoni vodka, Caesar salad prepared tableside, and veal parmesan. The restaurant is known for its upscale atmosphere and celebrity clientele.

Nobu

Nobu, located in Downtown Manhattan, is known for its high-end Japanese cuisine. Signature dishes include black cod with miso, yellowtail jalapeño, and a wide selection of sushi and sashimi. It is considered one of the top fine dining restaurants in New York City.

Sweets

New York City has many well-known bakeries, including Levain Bakery, which has locations in the Upper West Side and other neighborhoods. It is famous for its oversized chocolate chip walnut cookies, which are crispy on the outside and soft inside. Other popular dessert spots include Magnolia Bakery, known for banana pudding, and Dominique Ansel Bakery, famous for the cronut.
